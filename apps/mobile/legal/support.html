Common questions

How do I start a recording?

Tap the + button on the main screen to start a new recording. Tap stop when you're done. Twin will automatically transcribe and debrief your recording in the background.

Why is my recording still processing?

Transcription and debrief generation can take a few minutes depending on the length of your recording and your connection speed. Pull down to refresh the recordings list to check for updates.

How do I delete a recording?

Open the recording, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete Recording. Deletion is permanent and removes the audio, transcript, and debrief from our servers.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes all your recordings, transcripts, debriefs, and account data. This action cannot be undone.

Is my data private?

Yes. Twin only records when you explicitly start a recording — there is no background listening. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

The app isn't working — what should I do?

  • Make sure you have a stable internet connection
  • Force-quit the app and reopen it
  • Check that microphone permission is granted in Settings → Privacy → Microphone → Twin
  • If the issue persists, email us at support@twinai.co.site with a description of the problem and your iOS version
