Summary: Twin records audio you choose to record, converts it to transcripts and debriefs, and lets you chat with your day. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. Your recordings are yours.

1. Who we are

Twin is a mobile application operated by [Your Company Name] ("Twin," "we," "us," "our"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at [support@yourdomain.com].

2. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Twin mobile application and associated backend services (together, the "Service"). It does not apply to third-party websites or services we may link to.

3. Information we collect

A. Account information

When you create an account we collect:

  • Email address
  • A unique user identifier assigned by our authentication provider (Firebase Authentication)
  • Authentication tokens stored locally on your device to keep you signed in

We do not collect your name, phone number, address, or payment information as part of account creation.

B. Audio recordings and derived content

When you start a recording, your device captures audio through the microphone. We collect and process:

  • The audio file you record
  • Transcripts generated from your audio (text)
  • Speaker labels and timestamps (diarization)
  • AI-generated summaries, debriefs, and insights
  • Chat messages you send when interacting with your recordings

Recording only happens when you explicitly start it. We do not listen in the background.

C. Metadata

  • Recording timestamps, duration, and processing status
  • Consent acceptance and revocation timestamps
  • Voice profile data (if you enroll a voice profile for speaker identification)

D. Technical and diagnostic data

We may automatically collect limited technical data to keep the Service running:

  • App version
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • API request logs (request type, timestamp, status code — not content)

We do not fingerprint your device or build advertising profiles.

4. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the Service — process your audio, generate transcripts and debriefs, enable the AI chat feature
  • Store and display your recording history
  • Authenticate you and keep your session secure
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents
  • Improve reliability and fix bugs

We do not use your audio or derived content to train AI models without your explicit consent. We do not use your data for advertising.

5. Legal basis for processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, our legal bases are:

  • Contract performance — processing your audio and managing your account is necessary to provide the Service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — operating the Service securely and preventing abuse.
  • Consent — for any processing beyond what is necessary for the Service, where we have asked for your consent (e.g. in-app consent to process recordings).

6. Your recording responsibilities

Twin records audio only when you actively start a recording. You are solely responsible for ensuring that any recording you make complies with the laws of your jurisdiction and that you have obtained any required consent from other participants. Many jurisdictions require all-party consent for audio recording. Twin is not liable for illegal use of the recording feature.

7. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only as follows:

Service providers

We use third-party providers to operate the Service. These providers process your data on our behalf and are contractually prohibited from using it for any other purpose:

  • Google Firebase — authentication and user identity management. Firebase's privacy policy is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Cloud storage — audio files and generated artifacts are stored on our backend infrastructure (hosted on Railway/cloud servers).
  • AI transcription and generation — audio is sent to AI processing services to generate transcripts and debriefs. These services process your audio under data processing agreements.

Legal requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government request, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Twin, our users, or the public.

Business transfers

If Twin is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you beforehand via email or in-app notice.

8. Data retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Audio files — retained until you delete the recording or delete your account.
  • Transcripts and debriefs — retained until you delete the associated recording or your account.
  • Account data (email, UID) — retained until you delete your account.
  • Chat messages — stored locally on your device and on our servers, retained until you delete your account.
  • Logs and diagnostics — retained for up to 90 days for operational purposes.

You can delete individual recordings at any time within the app. You can delete your entire account from Settings → Delete Account, which will permanently delete all associated data from our servers.

9. Security

We use industry-standard protections including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit for all data transfers
  • Access controls limiting who can access your data
  • Authentication via Firebase for secure login

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your rights and freedoms, we will notify you as required by applicable law.
